Privacy Policy

Belzion LLC ("we", "us", or "our") operates the AI Knowledge Clipper Chrome extension.

Information We Collect

We only collect information that you voluntarily provide by clicking the "Clip" button on AI chat platforms. This includes the text content of AI responses you choose to save, along with any tags you assign.

How We Store Your Data

Free users: All data is stored locally in your browser. We do not have access to it.

Pro users: With your consent, clipped content is synced to our Firebase cloud service for cross-device access.

Data Sharing
